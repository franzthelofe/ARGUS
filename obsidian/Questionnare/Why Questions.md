###### Why build an autonomous system instead of relying on manual or remote-controlled search methods?
	To avoid risking the rescuers life, relying on traditional remote-controlled search method like rescue dogs can have its limitation like excessive training, and risking the dogs life.

###### Why combine survivor detection and hazard assessment into one system instead of two separate tools?
	Because seperating this two would be more complicated when operating, and additionally it waste much materials and time.

###### Why does this problem matter — what's the real cost of delayed or unsafe search operations?
	This problem matter because rescuers could be risking their lives going to the collapsed building or area.
