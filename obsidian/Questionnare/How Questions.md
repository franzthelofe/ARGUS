###### How does ARGUS distinguish a survivor from background debris or noise?
	ARGUS can use thermal heat sensor to detect the trapped survivors in the collapsed building.  

###### How does it move through unstable, unpredictable rubble without triggering further collapse?
	It moves very slowly due to its small size and this allows the robot to not cause any further damage to the collapsed buildings. 

###### How does it relay findings to human rescuers in real time?
	It uses thermal sensor to find survivors by their heat signatures and sending the data to the rescuers using their smartphone, by doing this rescuers can detect how many survivors in the collapsed building without going inside the building.

###### How will its accuracy and reliability be tested and validated?
	By conducting test operation and finding bugs to fixed to make it reliable as possible and safe for rescuers.
