###### What does ARGUS actually detect — survivors only, hazards only, or both simultaneously?
	It will detect heat signatures for survivors who are trapped in the collapsed building during the earthquake.

###### What sensors and technologies make detection possible (thermal imaging, CO2/gas sensors, cameras, sound or vibration sensing)?
	The sensors and technologies used are MLX90640 for thermal imaging, built-in camera from the ESP32, and lastly the sound sensor for the two-way communication.

###### What sets ARGUS apart from existing methods like rescue dogs or handheld search cameras?
	What sets ARGUS apart from rescue dogs is that rescue dogs can take much time to train, easier to drain their energy, and can waste their life.

###### What are its physical and operational limits (size, battery life, terrain it can't traverse)?
	The physical limit of ARGUS is the terrain it can't tranverse, the surroundings of the area.
