###### When in the disaster response timeline is ARGUS meant to deploy — e.g., within the "golden 72 hours" after an earthquake?
	The disaster response of ARGUS is when the rescuers arrived to the location of the collapsed building POST earthquake.

###### When is it expected to be field-tested or rescue-ready?
	When there will be no signs of flaws that can be dangerous during the test operation.

###### When can itoperate — day and night, or only under specific conditions?
	Depending on the condition if the earthquake struck on day or night.
